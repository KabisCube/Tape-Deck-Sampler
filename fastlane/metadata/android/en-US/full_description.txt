Tape Deck Sampler is an easy to use sampler with eight tracks and a sixteen-step sequencer with up to 16 pages. Each project and its samples are stored on a digital 48kHz mono cassette tape with a 20 second sample limit.

<b>Samples</b>
<ul>
<li>Record from the microphone or from other apps on Android 10 or later</li>
<li>Import audio files, or make sounds with text to speech and the tone synth</li>
<li>Trim each recording and place it anywhere on the tape</li>
</ul>

<b>Effects</b>
Stack effects per track or per pad: filters, delay, reverb, distortion, envelope, autotune, robot voice, choir, pitch, speed, wobble, swing, nudge, chance, every Nth pass and more.

<b>Clap sync</b>
Jam with friends without a network. Set the same tempo on every phone, select Sync and clap once.

<b>Export</b>
Export your sequence as WAV or MIDI, or the whole project to move it to another phone.

No ads, no tracking and no internet access.
